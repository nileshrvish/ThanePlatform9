# Platform No. 9 - Original Audio Pack: licences (Stage 8A, 2026-11-08)

## Summary

Every one of the 55 files in the Stage 8A original audio pack (`/Game/P9/Audio/OriginalAudioPack/`) was **generated from scratch by
code written for this project** (`P9_Assets/StationAudio/OriginalAudioPack8A/gen_8a_audio.py`). No external audio asset of any kind was
used: no samples, no recordings, no sound libraries (free or paid), no CC0 packs, no AI music or voice service, no TTS, no
third-party presets.

| Item | Source | Licence / rights |
|---|---|---|
| All 4 music tracks, 2 ambience beds, 31 sound effects, 18 creature sounds | Procedural synthesis in `gen_8a_audio.py` (sine/saw oscillators, filtered noise, modal "metal" and bell partials, a synthetic piano model, formant-filtered pulse trains for the scream and breathing, FFT convolution with generated reverb impulses) | Original work made for Platform No. 9. Owned by the project owner; no third-party rights. |
| Reverb impulse responses | Generated by the same script (exponentially decaying filtered noise) | Original, as above. |
| Tools used | Python 3.13 + NumPy (BSD licence), ffmpeg 8.1 (only for analysis / spectrogram pictures; LGPL/GPL tool, nothing of it is in the sounds) | Tools only - their licences do not apply to the output. |

## What the sounds are not

- **No voices.** Nothing in the pack is speech. The "whisper-like texture", "breathing", "distant call" and "scream" are
  filtered noise and pulse trains shaped by moving vowel-like resonances; they contain no words and are not recordings of a
  person. They must not be described as real voice recordings.
- **No imitation of existing works.** No melody, sound or motif was taken or recreated from any film, game or song.

## Not covered by this file

- The station announcements and NPC lines from earlier stages are **TTS (edge-tts) prototypes** (CLAUDE.md rule 7) - their
  licensing is still open and they were not touched in Stage 8A.
- The scanned people in the game (NPC models) need consent / store checks before a release (CLAUDE.md section 5).

## Commercial use

Because every 8A sound is original procedural output with no third-party input, the pack can be used commercially in Platform
No. 9 without attribution. Keep `gen_8a_audio.py` and `manifest_8a_*.json` with the project as the record of how each file was made.


## Stage 9A (2026-11-08)

The 55 Stage 9A files (`/Game/P9/Audio/OriginalAudioPack9A/`: footsteps, thunder, wind, train horn / running / braking) were generated
from scratch by `gen_9a_audio.py` (NumPy only) in the same way as the 8A pack. No recording, sample library, film / game audio, CC0 pack,
paid API or AI audio service was used; the horn is a synthesised pneumatic two-tone, not a recording or imitation of any specific
recording. Original work for Platform No. 9; commercially usable without attribution. Budget: Rs 0.


## Stage 9B (2026-11-08)

The 4 menu sounds (`/Game/P9/Audio/OriginalAudioPack9B/`) are procedural output of `gen_9b_audio.py` (NumPy, reusing the 8A/9A
synthesis code); original work, commercially usable, Rs 0. Caption text comes from the project's own scripts
(`announcements_mr.json`, `npc_dialogue.json`); the spoken lines themselves remain the earlier TTS prototypes (licensing still open).
